About Me

From YouTube and theater to video editing, motion graphics, animation, and multimedia design, I've always enjoyed turning ideas into something people can experience.

My creative interests span animated films, video games, horror, practical effects, art, coding, and stop-motion, often finding ways to bring them together in projects like Splatter Head.

My time at Walt Disney World has also shown me that storytelling extends beyond the screen, inspiring my interest in creating immersive experiences and memorable moments. Ultimately, I'm passionate about combining creativity, technology, and storytelling to make things people remember.

The One Man Band

I handle the full production pipeline myself. That means going from early storyboards and on-location filming straight through to the final cut with custom graphics, thumbnails, sound effects, and music.

Collaborative Professional

I love collaborating with others. Whether coordinating closely with clients or working alongside a team, I welcome feedback and hit deadlines so we deliver high-quality work without the stress.

Technical Problem Solver

I excel in fast-paced environments, offering immediate technical support and on-the-fly troubleshooting. I am highly analytical, ensuring workflows remain seamless while keeping a strong focus on details and organized record-keeping.
